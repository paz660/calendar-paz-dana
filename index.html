Calendar Paz&Dana

A private Android TV screensaver for one household. It shows upcoming events from two Google Calendar accounts on the living-room TV.

  • Read-only access (calendar.readonly). The app never creates, edits or deletes anything.
  • Calendar data is stored only on the TV device. There is no server and nothing is shared with anyone.
  • Not distributed publicly. Used only by the two account owners, each of whom connects their own account.
